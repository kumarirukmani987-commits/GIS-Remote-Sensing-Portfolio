# Certificates & Credentials

This section contains selected professional certificates and GIS training credentials.

## GIS & Remote Sensing

### 1. GIS, Mapping, and Spatial Analysis Specialization
University of Toronto | Coursera

A four-course specialization covering GIS mapping, GIS data acquisition, map design, spatial analysis, satellite imagery, and a GIS capstone project.

### 2. Fundamentals of GIS
University of California, Davis | Coursera

Course focused on fundamental concepts and applications of Geographic Information Systems.

### 3. Introduction to GIS Mapping
University of Toronto | Coursera

Course focused on introductory GIS mapping concepts and practices.

### 4. GIS Data Acquisition and Map Design
University of Toronto | Coursera

Course focused on GIS data acquisition and map design.

### 5. Spatial Analysis and Satellite Imagery in a GIS
University of Toronto | Coursera

Course focused on spatial analysis and the use of satellite imagery within GIS.
